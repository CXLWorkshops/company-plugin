# SEO / GEO Website Revamp Frameworks

Frameworks for restructuring a large content site for both traditional search and AI-driven retrieval. Mapped to the three workstreams in [[seo-geo cxl.com]]: archive, consolidate, GEO.

---

## 1. Content Audit & Pruning — *Archive & Cleanup*

**ROT analysis** — classify every page as **R**edundant, **O**utdated, or **T**rivial. Cleanest first filter for a large site; forces a binary "does this earn its keep" decision.

**Keep / Improve / Consolidate / Remove (KICR)** — the decision matrix each page lands in after the audit. Drive it with data:

- Organic traffic (last 12 mo)
- Impressions / clicks trend (GSC)
- Backlinks (don't archive a page with authority — redirect it)
- Conversions / business value
- Last meaningful update

**Trap to avoid:** pruning by traffic alone. A zero-traffic page with strong backlinks should be **consolidated / redirected**, not deleted. Directly counters the project risk "over-archiving valuable historical authority."

---

## 2. Consolidation & Cannibalization — *Workstream #2*

**Pillar–Cluster (hub & spoke) model** — concentrate authority: one pillar page owns the head term; cluster pages cover subtopics and link up. This is the consolidation target *state*, not just a cleanup tactic.

**One-intent-one-URL mapping** — build a keyword → URL map from GSC. Where multiple URLs rank for the same query, you've found cannibalization. Merge the weaker into the stronger, 301 the loser. Highest-leverage move for a bloated site.

---

## 3. GEO / AEO — *Workstream #3*

> Deep dive: [[geo optimization best practices]] — page-level playbook distilled from the 13-lesson CXL GEO course (Steve Toth). This section is the summary; that note is what the `aeo-geo-optimization` skill executes.

**GEO framework (Princeton / "Generative Engine Optimization" paper, Aggarwal et al.)** — research-tested tactics that measurably increased citation in generative engines:

- Adding citations to authoritative sources
- Direct quotations
- Statistics and concrete data
- Clear, fluent, authoritative language

What *didn't* help: keyword stuffing.

**Answer-first / chunk-retrievable structure** — operationalize GEO:

- Lead each section with a direct, self-contained answer (the extractable "chunk")
- Semantic HTML + descriptive headings phrased as questions
- Strong **entity clarity** (consistent naming of brand, people, concepts) for correct retrieval association
- Schema markup (FAQ, Article, Organization)
- TL;DR / summary block up top

**E-E-A-T** — the umbrella. For GEO it's less about rankings, more about being a *citeable* source.

---

## 4. Prioritization — *Which pages first*

**RICE / ICE scoring** or a simple `traffic × business value × opportunity` matrix. With traffic concentrated in the top pages (top 50 ≈ 65% of organic), protect high-leverage pages *before* the AI-search crunch. Don't optimize all pages equally.

---

## Through-line

Old SEO optimized to **rank a page**. GEO optimizes to **be the cited chunk inside an answer**. Treat extractability and entity clarity as first-class, not an afterthought to traditional SEO.
